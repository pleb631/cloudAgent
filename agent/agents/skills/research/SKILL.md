---
name: research
description: Evidence-based analysis of business documents in the workspace.
---

1. Clarify the decision or question being answered.
2. List relevant workspace files, then read the primary material.
3. Separate observed facts, assumptions, and recommendations.
4. Cite workspace filenames for claims and identify gaps in the evidence.
5. Return a concise conclusion with actionable next steps.

This skill does not enable network access. Never invent web research.
